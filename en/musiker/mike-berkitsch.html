Michael „Mike“ Berkitsch

Bass / vocals

Long established in Salzburg’s music scene with projects such as Atrium and BlueSmile, Mike Berkitsch also shared the enthusiasm for Balkan music from 2012 onwards.

His background in rock music and his ability in harmony vocals decisively shaped Ostbeatbend’s early years.

Mike Berkitsch left Ostbeatbend in 2015 for professional reasons.
