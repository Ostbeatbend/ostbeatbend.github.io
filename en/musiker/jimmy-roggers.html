Jimmy Roggers

Vocals / guitar / composition

Jimmy Roggers was born in Skopje, Macedonia, and now lives in Austria. As a singer, guitarist and composer, he has around 30 years of stage experience.

His music moves between country, blues and pop rock. His Balkan roots and southern temperament shape his distinctive style.

Their musical collaboration created a close connection with Ostbeatbend. Jimmy Roggers belongs to the band’s extended musical family and enriches joint performances with his experience, expressiveness and strong stage presence.
