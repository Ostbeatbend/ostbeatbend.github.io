Verena Aziz

Dance

Verena Aziz has performed and taught what is commonly called belly dance for many years. She enjoys incorporating influences from Indian and Afro-Brazilian dance, Roma dance, flamenco, modern dance and ballet, which also led to her collaboration with Ostbeatbend.

Her interpretation of Oriental dance continues to inspire both the band and its audiences.
