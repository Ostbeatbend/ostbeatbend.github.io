Aleksandra Milovanović

Vocals

Aleksandra Milovanović grew up in Serbia.

Singing is her way of expressing herself.

Music brought her to Vienna, where she attended the conservatory for solo singing. Since then, singing has been not only her profession but also the fulfilment of her dream.

Aleksandra has participated in numerous Serbian and international competitions in Belgrade, Ruma, Vienna and Ohrid, winning many first prizes.

Her very rewarding collaboration with Ostbeatbend began in 2021.
