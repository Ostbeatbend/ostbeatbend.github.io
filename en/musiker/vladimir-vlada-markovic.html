Vladimir „Vlada“ Marković

Bass

A true multi-instrumentalist.

Born in Serbia, he studied guitar, electric bass, double bass, keyboard, piano, viola and electric violin at the University of Music in Belgrade, Mozarteum University Salzburg, the Hanns Eisler School of Music Berlin and the Academy of Music and Drama in Gothenburg.

His musical engagements include Camerata Salzburg, Salzburg Chamber Soloists, Camerata Nordica, Gothenburg Symphony Orchestra, Largo di Garda Festival Orchestra and many more.

Vlada, who can readily be described as a musical prodigy, regularly appears as a guest with Ostbeatbend.

With his energy, joy of life and immense experience, he completes the Balkan groove on bass guitar.
