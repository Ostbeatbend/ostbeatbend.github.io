Nebojša Kisić

Guitar / vocals

A shared journey by Nebojša Kisić and Alex Zechbauer to the festival in Guča, Serbia, led to the founding of Ostbeatbend in 2012.

His love of many different musical styles, his extensive repertoire and his incomparable personal qualities had a profound influence on Ostbeatbend’s early years.

Nebojša Kisić passed away in 2016 after a serious illness.
